📚 语法总汇 · 整个小学阶段

不按某一册课本，按整个小学阶段（1–5 年级）把语法一次讲清：先打地基（26 个字母、10 种词性、缩略词），再学 4 个核心时态 + 2 个拓展时态和常用语法点，最后看句子结构、句子成分和 16 时态总览。带「初中拓展 · 先不用背」的只认个脸，不用背。写句子拿不准时，先查这里的速查表。

一条黄金规则（几乎每个时态都适用）：句子变成否定句或疑问句时，时态由小帮手扛着 —— do / does / did / will / have / has / be，后面的动词要变回原形。❌ He doesn't goes. / Did you went?　✅ He doesn't go. / Did you go?

① 先会变：动词的三种变形

每个时态都要用，先把这三套变形练熟，不规则过去式只能背。

三单 -s / -es

一般加 -s：play→plays；以 s、x、sh、ch、o 结尾加 -es：watch→watches, go→goes；辅音 + y 变 -ies：study→studies, fly→flies。

现在分词 -ing

一般加 -ing：sing→singing；不发音的 e 去 e 加 -ing：ride→riding, make→making；重读闭音节双写尾字母：swim→swimming, run→running, stop→stopping。

过去式 -ed

一般加 -ed：clean→cleaned；以 e 结尾加 -d：like→liked；辅音 + y 变 -ied：study→studied；双写尾字母加 -ed：stop→stopped。

③ 六个时态 · 一个一个看

一般现在时 General Present

经常、习惯、事实：反复发生的事和客观事实。

肯定：主语 + 动词原形（he/she/it 用动词 -s/-es）
否定：don't / doesn't + 动词原形
疑问：Do / Does + 主语 + 动词原形…?

时间标志词

oftenalwaysusuallysometimesneverevery day / weekevery timein the morningonce a weekhardly everevery Sundayat nightthree times a weektwice a month
  • I go to school every day. 我每天上学。
  • She watches TV every evening. 她每天晚上看电视。
  • Does he like apples? — Yes, he does. 他喜欢苹果吗？是的。
⚠️ he/she/it 后面别忘加 -s：❌ He go… ✅ He goes…；doesn't 后面用原形：❌ doesn't watches ✅ doesn't watch。

一般过去时 General Past

过去发生、已经结束，和现在没有关系。

肯定：主语 + 动词过去式（be 动词用 was / were）
否定：didn't + 动词原形
疑问：Did + 主语 + 动词原形…?

时间标志词

yesterdayjust now… agolast week / nightin the pastthe day before yesterdaylast timelast yearlast termlast Mondayonceonce upon a time
  • I visited a farm yesterday. 我昨天参观了一个农场。
  • She was at a camp last week. 她上周在野营。
  • Did you read a book last night? — Yes, I did. 你昨晚看书了吗？看了。
⚠️ was/were 不能和动词过去式一起用，did 后面不能留 -ed：❌ I was visited… / I didn't visited… ✅ I visited… / I didn't visit…

一般将来时 General Future

将要发生、打算做的事。小学只学两种结构：will 和 be going to。

肯定：主语 + will + 动词原形 / 主语 + am/is/are going to + 动词原形
否定：won't + 原形 / be + not going to + 原形
疑问：Will + 主语 + 原形…? / Be + 主语 + going to…?

时间标志词

tomorrowsoonnext weeksome dayin the futuretonighttomorrow nightthe day after tomorrownext timenext Fridaynext monthnext termsooner or laterat once
  • I am going to have a picnic tomorrow. 我明天要去野餐。
  • Tom will see a play this evening. 汤姆今晚要去看戏。
  • Are you going to visit Shanghai? — Yes, I am. 你要去上海吗？是的。
⚠️ will / going to 后面永远用原形：❌ I will goes / am going to went ✅ I will go / am going to go。

现在进行时 Present Continuous

正在进行：说话这一刻正在做的事。

肯定：主语 + am/is/are + 动词-ing
否定：am/is/are + not + 动词-ing
疑问：Am/Is/Are + 主语 + 动词-ing…?

时间标志词

nowright nowat the momentat this momentat this timeat presentLook!Listen!
  • Look! The children are having a running race now. 看！孩子们正在赛跑。
  • My mother is cooking dinner at the moment. 妈妈此刻正在做晚饭。
  • Are you doing your homework? — Yes, I am. 你在做作业吗？是的。
⚠️ be 要跟人称走（I am / he is / they are），-ing 不能丢：❌ They is playing. / I am read. ✅ They are playing. / I am reading.

过去进行时 Past Continuous ★ 拓展 · 五年级后看

过去某时正在进行：常作背景，另一件短动作在中间发生。

肯定：主语 + was/were + 动词-ing
否定：wasn't / weren't + 动词-ing
疑问：Was/Were + 主语 + 动词-ing…?

时间标志词

at that timeat that momentat 8 o'clock (last night)whilewhen
  • I was sleeping at 8 o'clock last night. 我昨晚八点正在睡觉。
  • While I was reading, Mum was cooking. 我看书时，妈妈在做饭。
  • When Dad came home, I was doing my homework. 爸爸回家时，我正在做作业。
⚠️ 短动作用 when + 过去时，长动作用 while + 过去进行时；已经做完的一次性动作不要用进行时：❌ I was visited the zoo yesterday. ✅ I visited the zoo yesterday.

现在完成时 Present Perfect ★ 拓展 · 五年级后看

过去发生、对现在有影响：结果或经历现在还重要；小学只用 already / yet / ever / never / just / so far，不钻研 for / since。

肯定：主语 + have/has + 过去分词 done
否定：haven't / hasn't + done
疑问：Have/Has + 主语 + done…? 答：Yes, I have. / No, she hasn't.

时间标志词

alreadyyeteverneverjustso farbeforeup to nowfor + 一段时间since + 过去某时间
  • I have finished my homework already. 我已经做完作业了。
  • She has never been to Beijing. 她从没去过北京。
  • Have you ever seen a panda? — Yes, I have. 你见过熊猫吗？见过。

过去分词先记这些： go→gone, see→seen, eat→eaten, do→done, take→taken, write→written, read→read, buy→bought, have→had, be→been, fly→flown, swim→swum, sing→sung, drink→drunk, give→given, find→found, lose→lost；规则动词的分词和过去式一样（finished, visited）。

⚠️ 不能说 have went / has saw，要用分词 have gone / has seen；ever 只用在疑问句和否定句里，不说 I have ever been… 这样的肯定句。

④ 两个易混对比

will 还是 be going to？

will：现在临时决定、承诺、帮忙，或自己猜测会发生。
— I'm thirsty. — I'll get you some water.（现在决定）
I think it will rain tomorrow.（猜测）

be going to：早就打算好的计划，或看眼前情况就能判断。
I am going to visit my grandma this weekend.（计划）
Look at the clouds! It is going to rain.（看得出来）

小学练习里两者大多可以互换，不用过度纠结区别。

现在完成时 还是 一般过去时？

先问一句：时间说清楚了吗？

说清了、而且已经结束（yesterday、last week、two days ago、in 2024）→ 用过去时：I went to Shanghai last week. / I lost my pen yesterday.

没说时间、重点是现在的结果或经历（already、yet、ever、never、just、so far）→ 用完成时：I have been to Shanghai. / I have lost my pen.（笔现在还没找到）

口诀：有明确过去时间 → 过去时；没说时间、强调现在的结果或经历 → 完成时。对话里常先用完成时开口，时间一出现就换过去时：— Have you ever been to Paris? — Yes, I went there last year.

⑤ 其他语法点 · 整个小学都要会

be 动词 am / is / are · was / were

I am / he, she, it is / you, we, they are；过去：单数和 I 用 was，复数和 you 用 were。否定在 be 后加 not，疑问把 be 提到句首。

I am a pupil. She is happy. They are in the classroom. He was ill yesterday.

⚠️ 一个句子只能有一个 be：不说 I am is…；be 要和主语对上，不说 They was…

have / has “有”

I / you / we / they have；he / she / it has；过去大家都用 had。否定和疑问借 do/does，后面用 have。

I have a new bike. She has two cats. He doesn't have a sister. Does Tom have a pen? — Yes, he does.

⚠️ Does 问句后面用 have，不用 has。

there be 某处“有”

There is + 单数或不可数；There are + 复数；过去用 was / were。be 跟离它最近的名词走。

There is a book on the desk. There are three pens in the bag. Is there any milk? — No, there isn't.

⚠️ “某处有”用 there be，“某人有”用 have/has：不说 There have a book。

名词所有格 's

单数名词和不以 s 结尾的复数加 's；以 s 结尾的复数只加 '。

Tom's book · the children's books · my father's car · the students' classroom

⚠️ 's 多用于人；物品多用 of：the door of the classroom。

名词复数 Plurals

一般加 -s；s/x/sh/ch 结尾加 -es；辅音+y 变 -ies；f/fe 变 -ves；土豆、西红柿等有生命的 o 结尾加 -es，photo、piano 加 -s。

box→boxes · family→families · knife→knives · potato→potatoes · photo→photos

不规则必背：man→men, woman→women, child→children, foot→feet, tooth→teeth, mouse→mice, goose→geese, fish→fish, sheep→sheep, deer→deer, Chinese→Chinese；people 本身是复数，一个人说 a person。

⚠️ 不说 childs、foots、mouses；不可数名词没有复数（见下面可数/不可数）。

冠词 a / an / the

a 用在辅音音素前，an 用在元音音素前——听发音，不看字母。the 指双方都知道的、再次提到的、独一无二的，或序数词、最高级、乐器前。

a book · a university（/juː/）· an apple · an hour（h 不发音）· the sun · the first · play the piano

不用冠词：球类和棋类、三餐、学科、星期/月份/季节、by + 交通工具、go to school。

⚠️ play football 不加 the，play the piano 要加 the。

指示代词 this / that / these / those

近处用 this / these，远处用 that / those；this、that 接单数，these、those 接复数。

This is a pen. That is a bird in the tree. These are apples. Those are birds.

⚠️ 单复数要对上：不说 This are pens / Those is a cat。

形容词和副词 Adjectives & Adverbs

形容词修饰名词：放在名词前，或跟在 be / look / feel / sound / smell / taste 后面。副词修饰动词：多放在动词后，很多以 -ly 结尾。

a beautiful flower · She is happy. · The soup smells nice. · run quickly · sing well · speak slowly

⚠️ good 是形容词，well 是副词：She is a good singer. She sings well. fast 两种都是它。

比较级和最高级 -er / -est

短词加 -er / -est（拼写规则和动词变形一样）；长词用 more / most；句型：A + be + 比较级 + than + B；最高级前加 the，后跟 in / of。

tall→taller→tallest · big→bigger→biggest · happy→happier→happiest · more interesting · Tom is taller than Jim. · Tom is the tallest in our class. · as tall as（一样……）

不规则：good/well→better→best, bad→worse→worst, many/much→more→most, little→less→least

⚠️ 不能 more 和 -er 一起用（不说 more taller）；than 是“比”，then 是“然后”。

介词 · 时间 in / on / at

at + 时间点：at 7 o'clock, at night, at noon；on + 某一天：on Monday, on 1st May, on Monday morning；in + 较长时段：in the morning, in March, in spring, in 2026。

⚠️ 记顺序：at（点）→ on（天）→ in（长时段），不说 in Monday / on 7 o'clock。

介词 · 地点 Place

in 在里面 · on 在上面 · at 在某地点 · under 在下面 · behind 在后面 · in front of 在前面 · beside / next to 在旁边 · between 在两者之间 · near 在附近 · above 在上方。

The cat is in the box. The book is on the desk. The dog is under the table. Sit beside me.

⚠️ 平的表面用 on：on the desk，不说 in the desk。

情态动词 can / may / must / should

can 会、能、可以；may 可以（更礼貌）、可能；must 必须，mustn't 禁止；should 应该。共同规则：后面只跟动词原形，不分人称，不加 -s / -ing / -ed / to。

I can swim. May I come in? — Yes, you may. You must keep quiet. You mustn't talk in class. You should listen to the teacher.

⚠️ 回答 Must I…? 表示“不必”要说 No, you needn't.，说 mustn't 就变成“禁止”了。

可数 / 不可数 some / any / much / many

可数：book, apple, pen（能数）；不可数：water, milk, rice, bread, juice, time, money, homework（不能一个个数的，没有复数）。

many + 可数复数，much + 不可数；a few + 可数，a little + 不可数；a lot of 两边都能用。some 多用于肯定句，any 多用于否定句和疑问句；量词：a cup of tea, two bottles of milk, a piece of paper。

How many books? How much water? I have some apples. I don't have any water.

⚠️ 礼貌请求时疑问句也能用 some：Would you like some juice?

What is this? — It's a book. Where is my pen? — On the desk. Whose bag is this? — It's Lucy's. How old are you? — I'm ten. How much is the toy bear? — Ten yuan.

⚠️ 语序是“疑问词 + 助动词/be + 主语 + 动词”：不说 Where you are?，要说 Where are you?

常用句型 Sentence Patterns

祈使句：动词原形开头（主语 you 省略），加 please 更礼貌；否定用 Don't + 原形。Open the box, please. Sit down, please. Don't walk on the grass!

提建议：Let's + 动词原形（答 OK. / Good idea.）；How about / What about + 名词或动词-ing?Let's go swimming. How about some juice? How about going swimming?

喜好与活动：like + 动词-ing / like to + 动词原形；go + 动词-ing 表去做运动或活动。I like swimming. Let's go fishing / skating / shopping.

想要：would like to + 原形 = want to + 原形，would like 更礼貌。I'd like to visit the museum. = I want to visit the museum.

这些先不要当小学重点学（初中内容）：过去完成时（had done）、现在完成进行时、将来进行时、过去将来时等其余时态，被动语态（be + done），双重所有格，关系代词引导的定语从句。它们在下面「16 时态总览」里只列名字，标着「初中拓展 · 先不用背」——小学先把上面 4 个核心时态学扎实。

⑥ 句子结构与成分 · 句子是搭出来的

小学先会前 5 种基本结构：看懂一个句子由哪几块搭成，造句时就不会漏块。第 6–8 种是从句，初中再学。

1. 主 + 谓　He runs. 他跑。　He runs quickly. 他快速地跑。
2. 主 + 谓 + 宾　I like apples. 我喜欢苹果。　They build a house. 他们建一座房子。
3. 主 + 系 + 表　She is beautiful. 她很漂亮。　He is an engineer. 他是一名工程师。
4. 主 + 谓 + 宾 + 宾补　She made John sad. 她使约翰伤心了。　They call me John. 他们都叫我约翰。
5. 主 + 谓 + 双宾　She shows me a book. 她向我展示一本书。　The teacher gives me his money. 老师把他的钱给了我。
初中拓展 · 先不用背 6. 名词性从句（主语从句、宾语从句、表语从句、同位语从句）· 7. 形容词性从句（就是定语从句 / 关系从句）· 8. 副词性从句（状语从句：时间、条件、结果、目的、原因、让步、地点、方式）——只认名字，不用背例句。

句子成分入门 · 先认这 4 个

主语 Subject谓语的描述对象、动作的发出者。
I eat bananas. 我吃香蕉。
谓语 Predicate主语做的动作或状态。
I eat bananas.
宾语 Object动作的对象、承受者。
I eat bananas.
表语 Predicative跟在系动词后，说明主语是谁、怎么样。
She is happy.
